## Operating Rule

Do not treat vibe marketing as "make it look attractive". Treat it as packaging a precise business opportunity signal for a specific B-end owner. Before producing any deliverable, identify:

- Target institution type: English, programming, robotics, science experiment, AI literacy,研学,综合素质, etc.
- Buyer: owner, campus principal, curriculum director, channel partner.
- Real operating pain: enrollment, renewal,客单价,成果交付, teacher delivery, parent-facing value.
- Fit boundary: who should not respond, so sales effort is not wasted.
- Platform and artifact type: Xiaohongshu note, Douyin video, Toutiao article/cover, content matrix, cooperation pitch, etc.

If the user has already approved a title, pain point, audience, hook, or visual direction, preserve it as locked strategy unless the user explicitly asks to change it.

When the user's topic already specifies target institution, buyer, platform, title, pain point, product, or delivery format, do not repeat the corresponding discovery step as a question. Instead, restate the known information in a structured brief, identify only the missing or risky assumptions, and continue with the next useful deliverable.

## Core Positioning

Treat 2B vibe marketing as a "business opportunity signal", not aesthetic decoration. The output should make the institution owner quickly feel:

- This is relevant to my current business.
- This can become a new growth/product line.
- The provider understands my operating pressure, not just the course content.
- Cooperation feels professional, concrete, and lower-risk.

Default master message:

> 帮培训机构快速拥有一套可招生、可交付、可出成果的科创赛考课程体系。

Use this distinction:

- Do not sell "a pile of courseware".
- Sell "a new business capability/product line" with curriculum, teacher training, enrollment material, project outputs, competition path, and operational support.

## Audience

Default target customers:

- English, programming, robotics, science experiment, AI literacy,研学,综合素质, or local education institutions seeking transformation.
- Institution owners, campus principals, curriculum directors, and channel partners.

Default anxieties to address:

- 招生难, 续费难, 客单价上不去.
- Existing courses are too interest-oriented and lack visible outcomes.
- Teachers can teach classes but cannot reliably support competitions, projects, defense, portfolios, or parent-facing成果展示.
- The institution wants to enter科创/白名单赛事/综合素质/科技特长方向 but fears high investment, unstable delivery, or weak parent acceptance.

## Lead Quality Filter

When adapting 科创赛考 content to institutions whose original business is not science/technology, especially English institutions, prioritize qualified leads over broad traffic.

Do not imply that 科创赛考 is an English competition, English upgrade class, or a natural answer to parents asking for English contests. That attracts mismatched leads and wastes sales effort.

For English institutions, target only owners who are already considering a second product line,综合素质 expansion,科技美育, project-based learning, AI/STEAM, or higher-value non-language offerings. Make the category shift explicit:

- This is not "英语比赛".
- This is a new "科技+美育+项目成果+赛考路径" product line.
- The English institution's existing value is only its campus trust, parent base, service capability, and expression-training experience.
- The cooperation question is whether the institution wants to enter 科创赛考 as a new business, not whether its English parents need more English competition options.

When writing cover titles or hooks, include a qualifier such as "想做第二增长线", "想转综合素质", "有老学员但缺新项目", or "想进入科技美育" when needed to filter the audience.

## Content Logic

When generating strategies or content, prefer these content pillars:

- 行业判断: why institutions should add 科创赛考 now.
- 机构痛点: describe a concrete operating problem.
- 方案拆解: show modules, paths, or implementation logic.
- 案例证明: use realistic pilot scenarios and outcome language without exaggeration.
- 合作邀请: invite trials, diagnostics, pilot camps, teacher training, or regional co-building.

For any post or short-video idea, make the implied funnel clear:

- 内容种草: trend and pain recognition.
- 资料领取: checklist, path map, manual, self-test form, parent meeting template.
- 诊断咨询: institution product-line diagnosis.
- 方案会: customized landing plan.
- 试点合作: 4-8 lesson trial, holiday project camp, competition camp, teacher training plus trial class.

## Voiceover Term Confirmation

Before estimating narrated-video timing or synthesizing speech, **must read and follow** [rules/speech-breath-design.md](speech-breath-design.md). Plan semantic breath groups, protected terms, connected clauses and speaker handoffs in a machine-readable breath plan. Synthesize continuous passages, measure actual pauses and listen to the complete audition before freezing a checksum-bound master. Natural phrasing is not proved by punctuation timing alone. If a speaking mouth is visible, its onset, resting intervals, re-entry and shot slices must follow the same frozen master; changing the breath timing invalidates affected mouth footage.

For rendered B2B promotional, sales, product-introduction, service-introduction, cooperation-pitch, or course-package videos, Chinese voiceover is required by default. A no-voiceover video is allowed only when the user explicitly asks for a silent, subtitle-only, poster-loop, background-screen, or visual-only version.

Do not treat unresolved terminology as permission to omit narration. If terms need confirmation, pause before synthesis and ask for term handling, or choose already-confirmed Chinese wording when the workspace/user rules provide it. The final promotional video should not be downgraded to moving slides with music unless the user explicitly approves that format.

Before generating Chinese voiceover, **must read and follow** [rules/voiceover-lexicon.md](voiceover-lexicon.md). Preserve the approved audience-facing copy, but create a separate conversational voiceover line before synthesis. Scan it for mixed Chinese-English terms, acronyms, brand English, product names, technical terms, numbers, dates, and polyphonic Chinese. Present a pronunciation/translation checklist for user confirmation when any unresolved choice materially changes what the audience hears.

For every video, this is a **video-design gate**, not a late TTS cleanup step: complete the conversational `voiceover_text` and a resolved pronunciation ledger before approving scene structure, estimating narration duration, selecting shot count, designing image assets, or rendering. The design draft must identify each polyphonic Chinese word by intended meaning/pronunciation and each English/acronym item by one chosen treatment: confirmed Chinese semantic replacement; Kokoro prohibits English and letter-by-letter reading. An unresolved item blocks timing and production; do not build shots around raw display copy and “fix the pronunciation later.”

For Kokoro voiceover, every English item must use Chinese meaning, including brands and acronyms; English pronunciation and letter-by-letter speech are prohibited. The canonical mappings and their context boundaries live in `rules/voiceover-lexicon.md`; do not maintain a second independent glossary here. Common examples include:

- `STEM` -> `科创融合课程`
- `PBL` -> `项目化学习`
- `GPT` -> `生成式人工智能工具`
- Semantic `AI` -> `人工智能`

Preserve visual brand spelling. For Kokoro, use the already-authorized Chinese meaning in the separate spoken text, without asking again; only unresolved meaning or a substantive rewrite needs clarification.
