# Xiaohongshu Copy Taboos for 2B Education Cooperation

These are mandatory requirements when creating Xiaohongshu copy, covers, editor's notes, captions, scripts, comment prompts, or publishing suggestions for 2B education partnership content. Treat a violation as a draft or delivery defect unless the user explicitly overrides the rule.

## Reusable Writing Standard

Before drafting each Xiaohongshu post, state briefly which numbered rules are being combined. Apply only the rules that materially help the post, but assess every draft against all ten:

1. Write to one specific person, not an abstract audience.
2. Find the real problem before writing the title.
3. Use plain human language instead of advertising jargon.
4. Give each post one clear point of view.
5. Make the opening three lines feel like “this is about me”.
6. Use concrete scenes instead of abstract claims.
7. Be sincere and credible rather than exaggerated.
8. Keep one post focused; do not stuff in unrelated themes.
9. Give readers one concrete takeaway: a method, judgment, checklist, or usable wording.
10. End with a natural, light product or service connection when appropriate.

Default publishing limits and style:

- Every publishable Xiaohongshu copy delivery must include a post title unless the user explicitly requests body copy only. If the user has approved a title, preserve it rather than silently rewriting it.
- The final Xiaohongshu post title should normally use **15–20 visible characters** so it has enough space to identify the target reader, business situation, conflict, or decision value while still fitting the platform limit. The hard maximum is **20 visible characters**. Count all Chinese characters, letters, numbers, spaces, punctuation marks, symbols, and emoji; punctuation and emoji are not excluded. A title shorter than 15 characters is allowed only when the shorter wording is demonstrably clearer or when the user has approved it; do not pad with empty adjectives, repeated punctuation, or unrelated emoji merely to reach 15. Treat a title over 20 characters as a delivery defect and shorten it before delivery.
- Keep draft body copy under 800 Chinese characters by default so the user retains editing room.
- Never exceed 10 hashtags. For the complete B2B platform package defined by this skill, deliver exactly 5 relevant hot tags unless the user requests another count.
- Use restrained, topic-aligned emoji in lists, section-like lines, and viewpoint summaries. For education content, prefer warm clear symbols such as 🎨, 🌱, 🏫, 📚, 🫶, ✨, 📝, and 🌈; avoid clutter or an excessively cute tone.
- For education-related publishable posts, add one natural closing sentence connecting the topic to “神笔马良AI智绘教室” or “AI智绘教室” when appropriate. Explain how the service could integrate with the need so schools or service providers remember it later; keep it subtle and service-oriented rather than a hard advertisement.

## Platform Risk Points

Avoid direct traffic diversion:

- Do not write phone numbers, WeChat IDs, QR codes, email addresses, external URLs, or other platform account names in正文、图片、视频、评论 or cover text.
- Do not use direct conversion phrases such as "加我", "联系我", "私信发联系方式", "看主页联系方式", "进群领取", "扫码", or "添加微信".
- Avoid disguised diversion terms such as "薇", "V", "卫星", "企鹅", "老地方", "你懂的", or similar evasive wording.

Avoid exaggerated promises:

- Do not claim "保证招生", "一定转化", "快速爆单", "稳赚", "零风险", "100%落地", "躺赚", "保底收益", or similar results.
- Prefer grounded language such as "先判断适不适合", "小规模试点", "降低试错成本", "跑通第一期", and "验证家长反馈".

Avoid fake proof:

- Do not invent case data, enrollment numbers, growth percentages, successful合作机构数量, or award results.
- If using examples without verified data, label them as a scenario, method, or trial path rather than a real case.

Avoid obvious interaction bait:

- Do not write "点赞收藏后发资料", "评论区打1领取", "转发给你方案", or similar engagement-for-resource prompts.
- Use softer platform-native endings, such as "可以先做一次产品线诊断", "先判断是否适合试点", or "适合的校区再进入方案沟通".

Avoid hard招商 language as the first touch:

- Use "试点", "共建", "产品线诊断", "课程体系合作", "交付能力", and "成果路径" before using words like "招商", "加盟", "代理", "赚钱", "爆单", or "高收益".
- If合作招募 is necessary, make it a later-stage post after trust-building content, and state screening conditions clearly.

## Content Quality Risks

- Avoid repetitive batch templates with nearly identical covers, titles, captions, or scripts.
- Avoid dense PPT-style text on covers. Use one strong signal and one filtering phrase.
- Avoid generic advertising tone. Xiaohongshu content should feel like判断、经验、避坑、方法 or真实场景分析.
- Keep one post focused. Do not stuff trend判断、产品介绍、招商条件、案例、价格 and合作流程 into one note.

## Safer Conversion Language

Use soft conversion prompts that invite self-screening rather than direct contact:

- "可以先判断科创赛考是否适合你的校区。"
- "适合的校区，可以从小规模项目营试点开始。"

- "能做，再进入方案沟通；不适合，也不要浪费成本。"
- "如果你的校区正在考虑第二增长线，可以先从适配判断开始。"

Do not force every post to convert. Some posts should only build category understanding or filter the audience.

Match the closing prompt to the post's actual pain point. Do not force every closing sentence into "第二增长线". If the post is about renewal, use wording such as "如果你也面临续费的痛点，可以先……"; if it is about enrollment,成果展示, teacher delivery, or客单价, adapt the first clause accordingly. The second half should still use a compliant self-screening action such as 参考试点路径, 做适配判断, 小规模试点, or 复盘家长反馈, rather than direct contact or resource-claim wording.

## 封面标题与发布标题分别撰写

封面标题（封面图上叠加的文字）与发布文案标题是两个独立字段，必须分别撰写、记录和检查，不能使用同一句标题。封面标题负责在信息流中以尽量简洁、快速、有冲击力的表达抓住视线，可使用噱头、反差、悬念、问题、认知冲突或业务机会信号；发布标题负责补足目标对象、具体场景、核心判断或阅读收获，通常写到15—20个可见字符。两者兑现同一内容承诺，不增加未经证实的利益点。仅改标点、空格、换行或字符大小写不算不同标题。允许9:16与4:3封面共用同一封面标题。

双标题必须形成可说明的**互补与反差关系**，而非同义改写。封面标题可以从意外判断、尖锐问题、反常识、损失风险、机会信号、事实、数据、政策方向或结果信号切入；发布标题再从目标受众、真实场景、原因机制、专业判断或可获得的决策收获切入。优先形成“**封面制造认知缺口 → 发布标题说明为什么值得看**”的连续兴趣，例如“**反常识/噱头 → 对象与机制**”“**问题/风险 → 判断与下一步**”“**事实/结果信号 → 营销判断**”。反差只能来自角度、语气和信息层次，不能让两个标题的事实结论互相矛盾，也不能用正文无法兑现的夸张承诺骗取点击。记录实际`titleRelationship`、`contrastMechanism`及其正文兑现位置；发布标题通常15—20个可见字符且硬上限20，不能为制造反差而扩写成第二段文案或加入未经证实的承诺。

交付前将实际封面文字与实际发布标题并排核对，去除空格、标点并忽略大小写后相同则不通过，再人工检查是否只是机械换词。已明确批准的同名组合不得静默改写：保留批准证据，指出冲突并提出最小改动候选，按现有决策锁确认后替换。单独交付文案如已有封面须读取实际文字；尚无封面则记录待配对，不声称整包通过。策略使用说明与逐条自检只存在生产记录，不混入可直接发布的正文。
