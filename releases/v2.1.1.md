# Vibe Marketing B2B Skill v2.1.1

v2.1.1 prevents approved product and evidence clips from drifting during later video revisions.

## Fixes

- Reuse the exact approved clip file and time range instead of independently selecting a new excerpt.
- Preserve the original aspect ratio and complete frame with whole-frame scaling.
- Interpret an approved `80%` scale as exactly 80% of canvas width, centered with 10% margins on both sides.
- Do not crop, zoom into, or enlarge a local detail of an approved clip.
- Keep clip overlays clear of Victor's face, mouth, and head silhouette; central placement may overlap hand gestures.
- Check the first, middle, and last overlay states and both shot boundaries before delivery.

## Upgrade notes

Record each approved clip's path, checksum, time range, aspect ratio, and scale in the project manifest. Existing videos do not need a contract-schema migration.
