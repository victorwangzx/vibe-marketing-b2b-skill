# Vibe Marketing B2B Skill v2.1.2

v2.1.2 improves vertical-video text hierarchy without changing the business-planning workflow.

## Typography update

- Title: 120 pt
- Body and supporting text: 60 pt
- Subtitles and captions: 40 pt
- Notes, sources, and remarks: 30 pt

Captions are now a dedicated production-contract token (`captionPx`) with their own line height. They may not inherit the body-text token.

## Upgrade notes

For an existing Remotion project, add `captionPx: 40`, a `captionLineHeight`, and `--caption-size` in the contract-to-CSS export; bind `.caption span` to that token; change `notePx` to 30; then lock the updated production contract before rendering.
